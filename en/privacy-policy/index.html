2. Your images are never collected

convertidorwebp.com is an image conversion tool that runs entirely in your browser using the HTML5 Canvas API. The JPG, JPEG or PNG images you select for conversion to WebP are never uploaded to any server, never pass through our infrastructure, and are never stored anywhere: processing happens entirely on your device and is discarded as soon as you close or reload the tab.

This means the owner never collects, stores, transmits, or has access to that content at any point.
