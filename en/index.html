Why convert JPG and PNG to WebP?

WebP is Google's image format built specifically for the web. It delivers superior compression — both lossy and lossless — meaning sharper images at smaller file sizes. This translates to faster websites, better Google PageSpeed scores, and lower bandwidth costs for your users.

WebP images are 25–80% smaller than their JPG or PNG equivalents with no visible quality loss. This makes converting to WebP one of the simplest, highest-impact optimizations you can make for any website. Our converter runs entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded to any server, ever.
