Why convert JPEG to WebP?

JPEG and JPG are the same format (JPEG is the full extension). WebP outperforms JPEG in compression: at the same visual quality, a WebP file is 25–50% smaller than its JPEG equivalent. For image-heavy websites, this reduction has a direct impact on load time.

Google recommends WebP as the primary image format for the web. Modern browsers support it at 97%+, and converting your JPEG files to WebP is one of the most effective actions for improving your site's Core Web Vitals score.
